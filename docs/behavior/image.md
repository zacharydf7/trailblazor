# Image

Pattern: none; it's a plain image

Familiar apps: none; it isn't a control

## Keyboard

Nothing of its own. It isn't a control.

## Pointer

Nothing of its own. An image that acts goes inside a link or button.

## Touch

Nothing of its own. It isn't a control.

## Focus

- [x] It takes no focus, and one with an empty `Alt` is left out of the accessibility tree. `ImageTests.Image_ShouldBeLeftOutOfTheAccessibilityTree_WhenItsAltIsEmpty`

## Not applicable

- Zooming or opening on click: a viewer is a different control.
