# Stack

Pattern: none; it's a layout helper

Familiar apps: none; it isn't a control

## Keyboard

Nothing of its own. It only arranges its items.

## Pointer

Nothing of its own. It only arranges its items.

## Touch

Nothing of its own. It only arranges its items.

## Focus

- [x] It takes no focus, and the tab order follows the markup in a column or a row, at every width. `trailblazor.css`

## Not applicable

- Reordering items: it has no reversed form, so what shows and what's read stay in the same order.
