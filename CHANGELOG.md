# Changelog

Trailblazor hasn't had a release yet. Until it does, two lists below run by date, newest first: renamed and removed
public API, and changes to markup and behavior that tests, styles or scripts can depend on. After the first release
they run by version. The docs site's Upgrading page is built from both.

### Markup and behavior

#### 2026-11-25

- `TbThemeHead` has `Nonce`, which it puts on its script and on a seeded palette's `<style>`, so a Content Security Policy needn't allow inline `<style>` elements. The new Content Security Policy guide gives the strictest policy the library works under. A dock panel's own window now copies a `<style>` with the nonce the page first loaded with, so a style with a nonce applies there too, even after an enhanced navigation.
- A `TbDataGrid` filter menu has an Add a condition button (`[data-part=filter-add-condition]`) for a second condition, joined to the first by And or Or (`[data-part=filter-join]`) and removed with a Remove button (`[data-part=filter-remove-condition]`); each condition sits in a `[data-part=filter-condition]`. A `GridFilter` has `Join` (`FilterJoin.And` or `FilterJoin.Or`), how it joins the filters before it on the same column; a `DataProvider` should honor it. New labels `DataGridLabels.FilterMatch`, `FilterAnd`, `FilterOr`, `FilterSecondCondition`, `FilterSecondValue`, `FilterAddCondition` and `FilterRemoveCondition`. The grid helpers' `FilterAsync(column, condition, text, join, secondCondition, secondText)` sets two conditions, and `FilterAsync(column, condition, text)` and `FilterAsync(column, text)` now remove a second one first.
- `TbDateTimePicker`'s time box, once interactive, is a text box like `TbTimePicker`'s (`[data-part=time-input]`, `role=combobox`, no longer `type=time`) that shows times in the `Culture`'s form and reads that form or 24-hour form, with a list of times by `Step` (`[data-part=listbox]`, each `[data-part=option]` with `data-time`) and a clock button (`[data-part=toggle]`, "Choose a time", `DateTimePickerLabels.OpenTimes`, `TbStrings.TimePickerOpen`); it no longer has a `step` attribute. Before the page is interactive it is still the browser's own date and time box. The driver and locator enter a time as before, and a test can now enter one in WebKit on Windows, whose Playwright build has no time box.
- `TbTimePicker`, once interactive, is a text box (`role=combobox`, `data-part=input`, no longer `type=time`) that shows times in its new `Culture`'s form and reads them in that form or in 24-hour form, with a list of times by `Step` (`[data-part=listbox]`, each `[data-part=option]` with `data-time`) and a clock button (`[data-part=toggle]`, "Choose a time", `TimePickerLabels.Open`, `TbStrings.TimePickerOpen`). The box's `data-value` holds the time in 24-hour form. Before the page is interactive it is still the browser's own time box. `TbTimePicker`'s driver and locator enter a time as before. Typing on an editable `TimeOnly` data grid cell now starts its edit with what was typed, where it used to just open.
- An editable `TbDataGrid` column of `DateOnly`, `TimeOnly`, `DateTime` or `DateTimeOffset` now edits with the matching picker: a date picker, a time picker, or a date-time picker for both `DateTime` and `DateTimeOffset`. Before, such a column needed an `EditTemplate`. A `DateOnly` column's custom `Format`, such as `yyyy-MM-dd`, is also its date box's format.
- In a `TbDataGrid` cell's or row's edit, including one from an `EditTemplate`, Enter on a button and any key in a popup, such as a picker's calendar, now go to that control instead of saving or canceling the edit. Tab in a cell's edit moves through its field's controls before it opens the next cell.
- `TbKanban` has `Lanes`, swimlanes across its columns as `KanbanLane` records with their cards in each column. Each lane's cell of a column is a list, `[data-part=cell]` with `data-lane`, named "{column}, {lane}", whose `FromList` and `ToList` in a move are the lane's `CellId` (`{lane}/{column}`, so neither id can hold a slash); the lane titles are `[data-part=lane]` in `[data-part=lanes]`, and the board gets `tb-kanban--lanes`. A card's Left and Right stay in its lane: a sortable list's Left and Right now go only to lists with the same `data-lane`, from the nearest element that has one. Both testing packages' Kanban helpers have `Lanes` or `LanesAsync`, `Cards(column, lane)` or `CardsAsync(column, lane)`, `Cell(column, lane)` and `MoveToColumnAsync(card, column, lane)`; their helpers that name only a column throw on a board with lanes.
- `TbKanban` has `Collapsible`, which puts a `[data-part=collapse]` button in each column's header that collapses the column to its title and count, and `@bind-CollapsedColumns` for the collapsed columns' ids. A collapsed column has `data-collapsed` and `tb-kanban__column--collapsed`, shows no cards or footer and takes none. The button's `aria-expanded` says whether its column is open, and `KanbanLabels.Cards`, or the string `Kanban.Cards`, names it ("Cards in {0}"). Collapsing or opening counts in `trailblazor.control.used` as `collapse` or `expand`. Both testing packages' Kanban helpers have `CollapseAsync`, `ExpandAsync` and `IsCollapsed` or `IsCollapsedAsync`, and read no cards in a collapsed column. A `TbSortable` that leaves its group and joins again, such as a column that opens, now takes its place in the other lists' move menus, in a Kanban the board's order.
- `TbCalendar` and `TbDatePicker` have `Pick`, a `CalendarPick` of `Day`, `Month` or `Year`. With a month or a year, the calendar opens on the months or the years and a pick there sets the value, held as the month's first day or January 1, or the first day `Min` allows in it. The date picker's box then takes the culture's short date without its day, or the year alone, unless `Format` is set. The calendar's grid and the date picker's box carry `data-pick` (`month` or `year`) for the testing helpers, whose `ChooseAsync` stops at the month or year. Each `[data-part=month]` button is now named by a visually hidden full month and year rather than `aria-label`, with its short name in an `aria-hidden` span, so its accessible name stays the same but its text holds both.
- `TbCalendar`, `TbDatePicker`, `TbDateTimePicker` and `TbDateRangePicker` have `ShowWeekNumbers`, which puts each week's number before its days, by the culture's rule in the Gregorian dates shown (ISO 8601 for a culture whose weeks start on Monday and whose first week is the first with four days of the new year), with one number for a week across New Year in both months. The numbers are row headers, `[data-part=week]`, under a column heading, `[data-part=week-heading]`, and the grid gets `tb-calendar__grid--week-numbers`. The strings `Calendar.WeekShort`, `Calendar.Week` and `Calendar.WeekNumber`, or the `WeekShort`, `Week` and `WeekNumber` labels of `CalendarLabels` and `DateRangePickerLabels`, word them.
- Calls to the app's data are traced: with a `TelemetryName`, a data grid's `DataProvider`, a tree view's `LoadChildren`, an autocomplete's or a mention input's `Search`, a rich text editor's `MentionSearch` and an async content region's `Load` each run in a span from the `Trailblazor` `ActivitySource`, named for the component and action, such as `TbDataGrid load`. Spans carry `trailblazor.telemetry_name`, `trailblazor.component`, `trailblazor.action`, `trailblazor.outcome` (`succeeded`, `failed` or `canceled`), `trailblazor.items` where there is a count, and the app's own tags, and a failure sets the error status and `error.type`. Nothing is started when no one listens or telemetry is off.
- `TbFileUpload` can send the files it chooses: `Upload` takes each file with an `IProgress<long>` and a cancellation token, and the field shows each file's progress (`[data-part=progress]`), a cancel button (`[data-part=cancel]`) while it runs, and its outcome (`[data-part=status]`), with a retry (`[data-part=retry]`) after it fails or is canceled. The cancel and retry buttons are the file's remove button (`[data-part=remove]`) changing its part, so focus stays on it, and a failed or canceled file also shows a remove button. `AutoUpload`, `MaxConcurrent`, `OnUploaded` and `FileTemplate` come with it, with `FileUploadItem` and `FileUploadStatus`; with `AutoUpload` off, an Upload button (`[data-part=upload]`) sends them. Each `[data-part=file]` now has `data-name`, and with `Upload` a `data-status`; the root has `data-uploading` while a file is sent or waits its turn. `FileUploadLabels` and the strings `FileUpload.Upload`, `Uploading`, `Cancel`, `Retry`, `Uploaded`, `Failed`, `Canceled`, `FileUploaded` and `FileFailed` word it. Both testing packages' file upload helpers read the names from `data-name`, and have `WaitForUploadsAsync`, `StatusAsync` or `Status`, `UploadAsync`, `CancelAsync` and `RetryAsync`. A failed `Upload` is counted in `trailblazor.control.failed` with the action `upload`.
- `TbDockManager` has `ActivePanel` and `ActivePanelChanged`: the showing panel of the group that people last pressed or focused, for a command such as Save. The dock's root has `data-active-panel` with its id, and both testing packages' dock helpers have `ActivePanelAsync()` or `ActivePanel`, which give its title.
- `TbDockPanel` has `TabEnd`, content after the title in the panel's tab, such as a count. It renders in `[data-part=tab-end]` and is part of the tab's name. The panel is then named by `aria-labelledby` on the title, `[data-part=title]`, so it keeps the title as its name. The Playwright dock helpers find a tab by its title part rather than its whole name.
- `TbDockManager` has `Locked`, which keeps the panels where they are: the menus offer no Move, New group, Auto-hide, Open in new window, Float or Dock; tabs and strip buttons don't drag; and a group whose menu would be empty shows no menu and no tab menu. The dock's root has `data-locked` while it is locked. Both testing packages' dock helpers have `IsLockedAsync()` or `IsLocked`, and their move helpers throw for a locked dock.
- A dock group's menu, and each tab's, have a Go to tab submenu after the moves that lists the group's tabs, when the group has more than one. Each item has `data-go-to` with its panel's id. `DockLabels.GoToTab` and the string `Dock.GoToTab` word the submenu.
- `TbSelect`, `TbMultiSelect`, `TbTreeSelect`, `TbMultiTreeSelect`, `TbDatePicker`, `TbDateRangePicker`, `TbDateTimePicker`, `TbTimePicker`, `TbColorPicker` and `TbTagInput` gain `Start` and `End`, and `TbCascader` and `TbAutocomplete` gain `End`, rendered in the box's `[data-part=start]` and `[data-part=end]` as on a text field. On a select, tree select, autocomplete, cascader, date picker, date range picker or color picker, the field's own buttons, such as its clear button, sit after the app's `End` content in `[data-part=end]`, and a color picker's swatch after its `Start` content. A date-time picker keeps its buttons with its boxes, before `End`. A link, input or other control in a select's or cascader's `Start` or `End` keeps its own clicks rather than opening the list. TB011 no longer leaves out an autocomplete with `Start` content, since a select has `Start` too. Pressing a tree select's box outside its trigger now focuses the trigger without opening it; a select's box already opened the select.
- A `TbDrawer`'s `Modal` and `Size` are now nullable, and when left unset they and `Width` follow the drawer's `Options`. All three always overrode the options before, so `Options` with `Modal = false`, a `Size` or a `Width` had no effect. With neither set, a drawer is still modal and `Small`.
- `TbDialog` gains `Modal`, `Size` and `Width`, which override the matching `Options` when set, as on a drawer.
- A `TbForm` given `OnSubmit` beside `OnValidSubmit` or `OnInvalidSubmit` now throws as it renders, as `EditForm` does. It used to raise only `OnSubmit` and never the other two.
- The analyzer has three new warnings. TB013 reports `TbDataGrid` parameters that don't go together, which throw as the grid renders: `Items` with a `DataProvider`; `Groups` with a `DataProvider`; a `ChildrenSelector` with a `DataProvider`, `Groups`, `OnRowMove` or a `PageSize`; and `Virtualize` with a `DetailTemplate`, with a `PageSize`, or with no `Height` or `Fill`. TB014 reports a `TbHotkey` whose `Keys`, written as text, can't be read, or are a key people type without `AllowCharacterKey`. TB015 reports a `TbForm` with `OnSubmit` beside `OnValidSubmit` or `OnInvalidSubmit`.
- New `TbValidationSummary`, inside a `TbForm`: while the form has messages, a `section.tb-validation-summary` named by its `h2[data-part="title"]` heading (the level from `HeadingLevel`; the string key `ValidationSummary.Title`, "Fix these to continue") with a `[data-part="list"]` of `[data-part="item"]` messages, each field's message as an `a[data-part="link"]` that moves focus to the field. `TbRadioGroup` and `TbCheckboxGroup` now always render an id on their `fieldset`, which a link points at. The testing helpers are `ValidationSummary()` in both packages.
- `IDialogService` gains `PromptAsync(title, label, initial, validate, ...)`, which opens a small `dialog` (not an `alertdialog`) with one labeled `TbTextField`, which takes focus and submits on Enter, and Cancel and OK buttons worded by `Dialog.Cancel` and `Dialog.Confirm`, and returns the text, or null when canceled. Its telemetry's `trailblazor.component` is `PromptDialog`; an app that implements `IDialogService` itself must add it. `FakeDialogService` gains `AnswerPrompt`.
- New `TbCopyButton`: a `TbButton`, or a `TbIconButton` with `IconOnly`, with the class `tb-copy-button`, `data-state` (`idle`, `copied` or `failed`) and `data-label` holding its words. It copies `Text` through `IBrowserService`, shows a check and "Copied" for two seconds (a check alone with `IconOnly`), or an error mark, "Couldn't copy" and the danger color when the browser refuses, and announces the result politely. Its root also carries `data-copies`, the count of finished copies. With a `TelemetryName` it counts `trailblazor.control.used` with the action `copied` or `failed`. The string keys are `CopyButton.Copy`, `CopyButton.Copied` and `CopyButton.Failed`, and the testing helpers are `CopyButton(label)` in both packages. `TbTextField` gains `Copyable`, which puts one at the field's end as `[data-part="copy"]`.
- `TbDateTimePicker` bound to a `DateTimeOffset` now shows it in the shared time zone (its own `TimeZone`, then a cascaded `TimeZoneInfo`, then `options.Times.TimeZone`, then the `TimeProvider`'s local zone) instead of in the value's own offset, and every value the user picks takes that zone's offset, where a changed value used to keep its offset. A value in another offset therefore shows a different clock time than before, and in a static form, which posts with no offset, an unchanged value moves unless the shared zone is the server's. A `DateTime` still shows as written.
- `TbText` gains `Truncation="TextTruncation.Middle"` (class `tb-text--middle`), which keeps a text's start and end. Text that `MaxLength` shortens now has its shown copy in `[data-part="shortened"]`; a middle cut with a `MaxLines` of 1 adds an empty `[data-part="middle"]` that its script fills, marking the text `data-middle-shown`. Copying shortened text by itself copies the whole text. Cut-off text with `ShowFullText` alone inside something that takes focus, such as a link, a button or a cell of a grid with `CellNavigation`, is no longer a tab stop of its own; focus on what holds it shows the tooltip. Directly in a table cell, text with a `MaxLines` of 1 now takes the column's width instead of widening the column. With `ShowFullText`, text that `MaxLength` shortens now has `data-measures`, so `IsCutAsync()` waits for its script. The testing helpers gain `ShownText` and `ShownTextAsync()`.
- New `TbCountdown`: a `div.tb-countdown` with `tabindex="-1"` and `data-state` (`running`, `paused`, `stayed` or `done`), holding a `[data-part="text"]` sentence and, while it counts down, `[data-part="actions"]` with its buttons. It announces its sentence politely as it starts and when it's extended, and again with 20 seconds left when it runs longer than 40 seconds. The string keys are `Countdown.GoNow`, `Countdown.Stay`, `Countdown.Stayed`, `Countdown.Seconds` and `Countdown.Minutes`, the last two each with a one-form such as `Countdown.SecondsOne`. It counts `trailblazor.control.used` with the action `go_now`, `stay`, `extend` or `elapsed`.
- New `TbDescriptionList` and `TbDescriptionItem`: a `div.tb-description-list` (with `tb-description-list--stacked` or `tb-description-list--inline`, `tb-description-list--columns` when `Columns` is above 1, and `--tb-description-list-columns` in its style) holding a `dl[data-part=list]`, whose pairs are `div[data-part=item]` around a `dt[data-part=term]` and a `dd[data-part=value]`. A blank `Value` holds the same dash and visually hidden "Not set" (`Value.Empty`) as a timestamp's null value. The testing helpers are `DescriptionList()` in both packages.
- `TbTimestamp` with `Format="TimestampFormat.Relative"` shows "in 3 days", "2 hours ago" or "just now", and keeps up on one timer for every relative timestamp on the page. Its `time` gets `tabindex="0"` and sits in a `span.tb-tooltip-anchor` beside a `span[role=tooltip][data-part=tooltip]` bubble that shows the exact date and time and describes it. The words are the string keys `Timestamp.JustNow`, plus `Timestamp.MinutesAgo` and `Timestamp.InMinutes` and the same pair for hours, days, months and years, each with a one-form such as `Timestamp.InMinutesOne`. A pointer press on a tooltip's trigger that isn't a control and sits inside none, such as a relative timestamp, cut-off `TbText` or an icon, now shows its tooltip, as on a button marked `aria-disabled`.
- New `TbTimestamp`: a `time.tb-timestamp` with the moment in ISO 8601 in `datetime`, or, for a null value, a `span.tb-timestamp.tb-timestamp--empty` holding an `aria-hidden` dash and a visually hidden "Not set". Its words are the string keys `Value.Empty` ("Not set") and `Timestamp.Zone` ("UTC{0}"), and `options.Times.Abbreviations` names zones for `ShowZone`. `TbChat` message times, `TbTimelineItem` times and `TbDataGrid` columns of `DateTimeOffset`, in their cells, date filter and exports, now show in one zone: a `TimeZoneInfo` cascaded around them, else the new `options.Times.TimeZone`, else the `TimeProvider`'s local zone (the chat's own `TimeZone` still wins). The timeline and the grid used to show each value in its own offset, so both can now show a different clock time for the same value, and the grid's date filter can match a different day. `IBrowserService` gains `GetTimeZoneAsync()`, which an app implementing it must add, and `FakeBrowserService` a `TimeZone`, UTC unless set.
- New `TbAsyncContent`: a `div.tb-async-content` with `data-label`, `data-state` (`loading`, `failed`, `empty`, `no-matches` or `content`), `aria-busy="true"` while a reload or a retry runs, and `tabindex="-1"`. It holds an always-present `[data-part="status"]` with `role="status"`, which shows the loading words and the spinner while a first load is slow. With `LoadingContent`, that content takes the spinner's place and the status is visually hidden. It also holds `[data-part="loading"]` for `LoadingContent`, `[data-part="progress"]` while a reload is slow, and `[data-part="failed"]` with its `[data-part="retry"]` button. After a Retry that loads, focus moves to the region. The string keys are `AsyncContent.Loading`, `AsyncContent.LoadFailed` and `AsyncContent.Retry`, and the testing helpers are `AsyncContent(label)` in both packages. Each load is recorded in the new `trailblazor.load.duration` histogram with the outcome `loaded` or `failed`, a failure in `trailblazor.control.failed` with the action `load`, and a Retry in `trailblazor.control.used` with the action `retried`.
- `TbButton`, `TbIconButton`, `TbFab`, `TbToolbarButton`, `TbRibbonButton` and `TbMenuItem` take a `DisabledReason`. While a button with a reason is off, it stays focusable with `aria-disabled`, and `aria-describedby` points at a hidden span holding the reason. The button also gets the class `tb-button--reason` and holds a tooltip of its own, a `[data-part="tooltip"]` bubble with `aria-hidden="true"` that shows the reason in a `.tb-tooltip__line` span. For an icon button with `ShowTooltip`, a line with the name comes first, and the icon button's usual `TbTooltip` is off while the reason shows. An icon-only toolbar button shows both lines in its usual name tooltip instead, whose bubble is then `aria-hidden`. With a `TelemetryName`, a press on a button that's off with a reason counts `trailblazor.control.used` with the action `pressed_off`. A disabled menu item, including a toolbar button's item in the overflow menu, shows the reason in `data-part="description"` in place of its `Description`, and counts nothing when pressed. On a static page, a submit button marked `aria-disabled` no longer posts its form, whether or not the form is a `TbForm`.
- `TbTooltip` takes `Describes`; when it's false, the trigger gets no `aria-describedby` from the tooltip and the bubble has `aria-hidden="true"`, and a tooltip that stops describing its trigger takes its id back. A pointer press on any trigger marked `aria-disabled` now shows its tooltip instead of hiding it, including a busy, cooling-down or `FocusableWhenDisabled` button, and a pointer press outside a tooltip that a pointer showed, by hover or by a press on a trigger that's off, hides it; a tooltip shown for keyboard focus stays until focus leaves.

#### 2026-11-24

- The bUnit helpers' names now leave out content inside a `hidden` element, as a browser does.
- A `TbButton` with the new `Cooldown` ignores clicks for that long after its `OnClick` finishes without throwing. It stays focusable with `aria-disabled`, shows the seconds left in `data-part="cooldown"` after its text, and `aria-describedby` points at a hidden description of the seconds left, such as "Available again in 30 seconds", which is announced once as the cooldown starts.
